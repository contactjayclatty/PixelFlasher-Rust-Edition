The rule

Nothing runs until you ask.

The app does not scan on launch, and it does not embed the Python original. A device write stays a frozen plan until you review the target and type that device's confirmation.

01

Scan, then select

Platform-tools stay quiet until you press Scan. Pick the device before you prepare anything.

02

Read the plan

Factory, OTA, image, and live-boot operations show the commands, files, and choices before they run.

03

Confirm the device

Destructive work asks for a typed confirmation tied to that phone. Stopping later cannot undo a write that finished.
